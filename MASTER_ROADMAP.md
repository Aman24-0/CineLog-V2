# 🎬 CineLog V2 Master Roadmap
### Version: Industry Roadmap v1.0
### Status: Living Document
### Vision:
Build the world's best personal movie & TV tracking platform that combines the strongest parts of Letterboxd, Trakt, Simkl and TV Time while creating its own unique identity through Universes, Collections and a cinematic experience.

---

# Philosophy

CineLog is NOT a streaming app.

CineLog is NOT another TMDB client.

CineLog is a premium personal entertainment operating system.

Every feature must answer one question:

> "Does this help people organize, discover, remember or enjoy movies and TV better?"

If the answer is no,
don't build it.

---

CineLog should become known for:
